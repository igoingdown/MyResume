## Where to Add New Code

**Adding a new resume section:**
1. Insert a `\section{Section Name}` block in `main.tex` between existing sections
2. Use `\cventry{date}{org}{dept}{role}{award}{description}` for each entry
3. Place before `\newpage` (line 127) for page 1-2 content, or after for page 3

**Adding a new work/internship entry:**
- Insert a new `\cventry{...}` block within the appropriate `\section{工作经历}` or `\section{实习经历}` block

**Updating contact info:**
- Edit preamble macros at lines 11-19 of `main.tex`

**Changing photo:**
- Replace `photo.png` in project root (must keep same filename, or update line 19 reference)

**Modifying styling:**
- Change theme/color at line 3: `\moderncvtheme[blue]{classic}`
- Adjust margins at line 8
- Modify accent color: find/replace `\textcolor[RGB]{64,127,191}` throughout
